Mark

Find the moments. Mark them in seconds.

Play your video and press I and O. Each range becomes a clip you can trim, rename and reorder on the timeline.

  • Thumbnails and waveform so you see where the action and the talking are.
  • Markers for ideas you want to come back to.
  • Projects save themselves, and recover if Windows closes the app.

Frame

One video, framed for the phone.

Crop the speaker, split two people top and bottom, or place the whole frame on a background. Each clip keeps its own framing.

  • Fill, Split, Trio and On background layouts.
  • Five shapes: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9.
  • Backgrounds: blur, colour, gradient or your own picture.

Questions

Is Fragmio free?

Yes. Everything is free during the beta. Later there will be a free version and a Pro version with extras.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Fragmio only connects when you click Help → Check for updates, and then it only reads a small version file.

Which videos can I use?

MP4 videos from your camera, phone, screen recorder or streaming software, up to 4K.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that haven't built a reputation yet. Click More info → Run anyway. It will go away as more people use Fragmio.

Do I need a powerful computer?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and 8 GB of memory. An NVIDIA graphics card makes exports faster but isn't required.

How do I report a problem?

In the app, choose Help → Report a problem. The form opens in your browser with your version filled in.
