How it works

From a one-hour recording to a week of posts.

  1. Open your video
    Pick an MP4. Fragmio shows it on a timeline with the sound wave.
  2. Mark the clips
    Press I and O to mark each moment. Make as many as you like.
  3. Frame and style
    Choose the layout, add titles and captions, or apply a template.
  4. Export
    All clips export together, ready to upload.

Your videos stay yours
